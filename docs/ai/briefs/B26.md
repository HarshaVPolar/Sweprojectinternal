## Contract (docs/design/build-contract.md)

- Specification: build contract section 8.4; build contract section 9 (find_similar_videos and summarise_video); build contract section 11 (video score); docs/api/tools.md section 3.1 and docs/api/tools.md section 3.7; docs/design/scoring.md section 4 and docs/design/scoring.md section 6; docs/design/provider.md section 8 (fixture fallbacks).
- SimilarityService(provider, guard, cache, clock, settings). similar_videos(video, limit, filters) -> list of results with components: normalise the ID; plan videos_list for the uncached seed, search_list x similarity.fr1_search_queries (more pages when candidates exceed 50) and videos_list for the uncached candidates; check the guard; fetch the seed (missing or private gives NotFoundOrPrivate); query = the top 6 terms of docs/design/scoring.md section 6; search up to similarity.default_candidates candidates; exclude the seed; fetch the candidates; filters max_duration_s, language, published_after, exclude_channels and dropping non-public, live and unavailable videos are applied locally; rank_videos; return at most limit. A seed without tags adds the notice "Seed has no tags: tags ignored, weights renormalised." Results are cached (search results 24 hours): a second identical request costs 0 units and returns the identical order.
- summarise(video) -> a metadata summary with chapters taken from description lines that start with a timestamp (m:ss or h:mm:ss) followed by a title, accepted only when there are at least 3 in ascending order; the text states that the summary is based on metadata.
- tools/find_similar_videos.py and tools/summarise_video.py render the envelope with sanitised and delimited text and the url https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=<id>. server.py and ToolDeps get the new fields.
